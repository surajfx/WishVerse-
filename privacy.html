WISHVERSE · DRAFT POLICY

Privacy Policy

Development draft — review and update before public launch.

Information you provide

WishVerse may process recipient and sender names, messages, relationship details, uploaded photos and scheduled unlock settings that you enter while creating a keepsake.

Storage and service providers

The current development code is configured to store wish documents with Firebase Firestore and upload images through Cloudinary. Their applicable privacy terms and your project configuration also matter.

Shared links

Anyone with a shared keepsake link may be able to view its content depending on the current database rules. Do not include confidential or sensitive information. Scheduled unlock in the current prototype is a client-side experience, not a security boundary.

Before launch

This is a starter policy, not a complete legal document. Add accurate contact details, retention and deletion procedures, and review applicable legal requirements before making WishVerse public.
